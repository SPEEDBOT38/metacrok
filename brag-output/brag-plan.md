# Brag Plan: metacrok

## What is this app?
metacrok reads every live Meta ad your rivals are running (up to 15 each, straight from the Ad Library), maps where the whole niche crowds, and writes three ads aimed at the space nobody owns — for solo D2C founders, in about 90 seconds. The joke is the mascot: a gator who literally chomps rival ads (the hero is a playable croc game with an "ADS CHOMPED" HUD).

## The angle
Arcade gator devours your rivals' ad strategies, then hands you the loot. The video plays the product's own metaphor completely straight: a pixel-HUD croc eats a pile of rival ad cards, "chews" (the app's real progress step), and spits out three finished ads — the exact Feed → Chew → Fresh out arc the product uses. Retro Press Start 2P HUD energy layered over the real modern product UI. Specific to metacrok because every asset (croc sprites, ad-chomp HUD, the finding panel, the three sample ad cards) comes from the product itself.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A wobbling stack of rival ad cards slides in and the croc sprite CHOMPS through it while a pixel HUD counter spins up: `ADS CHOMPED 0045`. Text lands after the bite: "your rivals ran 45 ads."

## Key moments (the middle)
- The feed form with simulated typing: brand "glowandco", three rival chips, cursor clicks the orange "Feed the croc" button.
- The Chew: progress lines ticking ("pulling 15 live ads × 3 rivals" → "reading every ad" → "mapping the niche") with the ad-chomp counter still climbing and ad cards streaming past the croc.
- The money shot — "fresh out": the verdict line with yellow highlight ("nobody in skincare explains **how the ingredients work**"), the stat chips (30 rival ads read · 47% shout pain points · 0 teach), then the three sample ad cards sliding in one by one (education / problem-solution / value), exactly as the site renders them.

## Outro / punchline
Giant "Hungry?" on arcade blue with the croc running in, then the lockup: "eats ads, spits out better ones" + "Feed the croc — 90 seconds, no sign-up" + metacrok logo.

## User flow worth showing
Feed (name your brand + up to 3 rivals) → Chew (~90s live progress while he reads everyone) → Fresh out (verdict, positioning map, three paste-ready ads). The centerpiece scenes 2-4 walk this exact flow; the ad cards in scene 4 are the product's real output format.

## Tone
- Preset: `default`
- Creative direction: "arcade gator devouring your rivals' ad strategies — pixel HUD energy over real product UI"
- Interpretation: playful and fast, but the product moments (form, progress, result cards) are recreated faithfully and given room to read; humor comes from the chomp metaphor, not wackiness in the UI scenes.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20.5s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #0B192C (deep navy; panels #12243C; arcade blue #1150AB)
- Accent: #F7862A (orange CTA) with #FFD23A / #FFDE59 yellow highlight and #37B7C3 teal outlines
- Text: #F5F8FC (muted #C9D6E6)
- Display font: Oxanium 800, lowercase, tight letter-spacing, hard offset shadow (#0B192C)
- Body font: Funnel Sans; pixel accents in Press Start 2P
- Strongest visual element: the croc game sprites (crocRun1/2, crocJump, crocChompOpen/Bite) chomping the product's ad cards, plus the pixel "ADS CHOMPED" HUD; ad cards styled as the site renders them (white card, avatar dot, Sponsored, big color block with lowercase Oxanium line, footer bar with CTA)

## Share copy (draft)
Your rivals ran 45 ads. Our gator read every one, found the gap nobody owns, and wrote your next three. metacrok — eats ads, spits out better ones. https://metacrok.vercel.app

## Audio direction
- Role: dense rhythmic layer under the arcade scenes, easing into a confident bed under the product scenes
- Music: bundled happy-beats-business-moves pack (pick the most upbeat playful volume with a cue preset)
- Music treatment: start at 0s at full energy, duck slightly under the verdict line in scene 4, hard ending on the logo hit
- Music cue guidance: read the chosen track's preset from the skill's `assets/music/cues/`; target strong cues for the scene-1 chomp, the scene-4 card sequence (snap card arrivals to the beat grid but hold each card ≥1.2s settled), and the outro logo
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; HUD counter and card glow may breathe with the beat, no waveform bars
- SFX posture: moderate, motion-matched — chomp/bite impact on scene-1 bites, UI ticks on typing and chips, soft whoosh on card arrivals
- Audio-coupled moments: HUD counter ticks, typing in scene 2, click on "Feed the croc", beat-aligned card-by-card reveal in scene 4
- Restraint rule: SFX never talk over the verdict line; the scene-4 card sequence may be beat-snapped in arrival but each card holds long enough to read

The croc sprite runs in from the left (crocRun1/2 cycle) and CHOMPS (crocChompOpen → crocChompBite); the stack collapses into crumbs/pixels; pixel HUD top-right ticks 0000 → 0045 in big #FFDE59 Press Start 2P. Then the line slams in, Oxanium lowercase with hard shadow: "your rivals ran 45 ads."
Sequential/interaction: yes — stack slides in, two bites, counter ticks up through each bite

Audio-coupled idea: counter ticks synced to bites; card-stack shake on each bite
