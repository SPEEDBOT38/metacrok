What we won’t pretend.

  • No spend, no ROAS. Meta doesn’t publish them for commercial ads. We use how long an ad has run and how many versions exist, and we call that a hint, not proof.
  • No invented claims. Ratings, discounts, certifications, founder stories and rival names become placeholders unless you gave them to us.
  • No copying. Any line that repeats a rival’s wording is replaced. We borrow structure, never words.
  • No posting for you. Metacrok never touches your ad account. You review, then paste into Ads Manager.
